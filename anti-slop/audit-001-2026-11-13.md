# antislop audit 001 (2026-11-13)

Scope: the whole dashboard (`index.html`, `css/`, `js/`). Direction: keep the current identity (dark surfaces, Strava orange `#FC4C02`), polish only.

Method:
- `npx impeccable detect` static scan over `index.html css js` (94 findings)
- headless Chrome on a local `dist/` build in demo mode (140 sample activities), every sidebar section shot at 1440 px and at 390 px phone width
- contrast ratios computed from the real tokens in `css/base.css`

Demo data caveat: the demo dates are not in order, so the zig-zag x axis on "Avg speed, last 20 rides" is a demo artefact, not a finding. Cards that need activity streams (decoupling, time lost, wind) can only show their empty state in demo mode, so #8 needs a check against live data.

Nothing has been changed yet. Pick the numbers to fix; numbers not picked stay untouched.

## HIGH (Hard Gate)

1. **Secondary text fails contrast (R-25).** `--text-3 #555` is 2.5:1 on cards, `--muted #666` is 3.0 to 3.2:1, and `--dim #3a3a3a` is about 1.7:1. They carry real information: the caption under every stat ("all time", "avg 54.2 km"), sidebar group labels, the search placeholder, chart footnotes. The scan counts over 200 text nodes on these colors. Fix: raise `--text-3` to `#8a8a8a` (5.4:1) and `--muted` to `#8f8f8f` (5.8:1), and keep `--dim` for borders and dividers only.
2. **White on orange fails contrast on filled buttons (R-25).** `#fff` on `#FC4C02` is 3.4:1 at 13 px bold (Refresh, Story, Install App, active EN / km / sport toggles). Fix: a deeper fill for filled controls only (`#D93F00`, 4.5:1). Orange as text, icons, charts and borders stays `#FC4C02`, which reads 5.9:1 on the page.
3. **Keyboard focus is invisible on 9 controls (R-32).** These set `outline: none` with no replacement: `.seg-sort`, `.gr-search`, `.gr-select`, `.ai-goal`, `.ai-input`, `.ai-select`, `.ai-model`, `.ai-cap-input`, `.gc-rp input` (`css/sections.css`). There is no global `:focus-visible` style either. Fix: one `:focus-visible` rule in `base.css` (2 px orange ring) and drop the bare `outline: none`.
4. **Motion ignores reduced-motion (R-39).** The only `prefers-reduced-motion` rule covers the 3D-view spinner. The infinite `aiBlink` dots, the pulsing online dot and the chart/scroll animations keep running. Fix: one global reduced-motion block.
5. **Text below 11 px (R-39, R-03).** 46 functional labels are 9 to 10.5 px: sidebar group labels (PERFORMANCE / ACTIVITIES / EXPLORE at 9 px), activity type tags ("Ride", "GravelRide"), chart footnotes. Fix: an 11 px floor.
6. **108 em dashes in UI text (R-02).** Examples: "Independent app — not affiliated with Strava", "Demo mode — 140 sample activities", "Bulk caption tools — rewrite titles", "Estimated — each activity's moving time". Most are in `i18n.js` strings, so the Indonesian side needs the same pass. Fix: commas, colons or full stops.
7. **The floating AI / Image / Story buttons cover content (R-03).** On the phone, the three buttons plus the "Install app" pill sit over the Eddington and Rides stat cards. On desktop they sit over the right-hand stat card and chart corners. Fix: reserve bottom padding on `main` equal to the button stack, and collapse to a single Story button with a menu on phones.
8. **Dead-end cards at the end of Training (R-27).** "Heart rate decoupling", "Time lost analysis" and "Wind analysis" (shows only loading dots) end the page with "Couldn't load stream data". "Segment intelligence" only explains that it runs elsewhere. Fix: one honest empty state per card with what would fill it, or hide a card when it has nothing for this ride. Needs a check on live data first.
9. **Broken `<img src="">` (R-39).** Three images ship with an empty `src` (sidebar avatar and two others) until data loads, which fires a request for the page itself. Fix: no `src` attribute until there is a URL, plus `alt`.

## MEDIUM (Purpose-Gate)

10. **Overview is 25 identical tiles with no focal point (R-14, R-05).** Distance, time and elevation carry the same weight as Pizza slices, Around the equator and Everests. Every tile is icon + tracked capitals + number, five across. Fix: a hero row of 3 to 4 key numbers set larger, then the rest in a tighter secondary grid, with the novelty stats grouped as one "fun facts" strip.
11. **Colored left stripes (side-tab, Part 1).** These are on 7 CSS rules (`cards.css:218`, `sections.css:739, 1060, 1561, 1734`, `sleep.css:64, 379`), plus the orange-barred explainer note under almost every Training card. Fix: drop the bars and set explainers as a plain muted caption.
12. **Orange glow on 6 elements (R-13).** These are box-shadow glows on buttons/FABs/cards plus a zero-offset text-shadow glow. Fix: keep it on the primary Story button only, or nowhere.
13. **Tracked capitals everywhere (R-06).** Every card title and stat label is uppercase with wide tracking ("RECENT ACTIVITIES", "TIME IN HEART RATE ZONES", "LONGEST RIDE"). Fix: keep tracked caps for the sidebar group labels and section rules only, and sentence case for card titles.
14. **Recovery + Day Strain shown twice (C-3).** The same two cards head both Overview and Training. Fix: keep them on Overview; Training starts at its own content.
15. **Training is one 4,500 px scroll of about 22 cards (C-3, R-05).** There is no in-page way to reach the PMC chart, climbing or ride quality. Fix: a sticky sub-nav (Load · Zones · Readiness · Trends · Latest ride) or collapsible groups.
16. **Activities bubble chart adds little (C-3, R-25).** It shows bubbles labelled only by km, in dark-red text on dark brown at about 2:1, and you can't click or hover them. Fix: link each bubble to its activity and lift the label contrast, or replace it with something that answers a question (distance by week, for example).

## LOW (Quality Locks)

17. **Header crowding (R-20).** Language, sport filter, units, Disconnect and Refresh all sit in the top bar at equal weight, with Disconnect right beside Refresh. Fix: move language, units and Disconnect into Settings.
18. **Landing page details (R-39, R-06).** There are skipped heading levels (h1 to h4, h2 to h4), and the hero headline uses -0.09em tracking at 16 px. Fix: correct the heading order and ease the tracking.
19. **Repeating-stripes decorative gradient (R-07).** One block uses a repeating stripe background with no stated purpose. Fix: remove it or write the reason.
